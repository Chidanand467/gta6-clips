GTA6 Clipper

An automated highlight-clipping tool for one gaming YouTube channel.

What GTA6 Clipper does

GTA6 Clipper is a personal automation tool, built and run by its developer (GitHub user chidanand467) for their own YouTube Shorts channel about the video game Grand Theft Auto VI. It:

  1. Checks the public stream recordings (VODs) of a small list of gaming streamers who have given the channel owner written permission to clip their streams.
  2. Finds exciting moments in those streams using audio loudness and speech transcription.
  3. Cuts each moment into a short vertical video (25 to 55 seconds) with captions and an on-screen credit naming the original streamer.
  4. Uses an AI text model to suggest a title and description from the clip's spoken transcript.
  5. Uploads the finished clip to the channel owner's own YouTube channel, with a link back to the streamer's channel and the original stream.

Why it uses Google user data

GTA6 Clipper requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload ("Manage your YouTube videos" – upload). It uses this permission only to upload and schedule the clips described above on the channel owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, change or delete any other data in the Google account.

The only person who signs in is the channel owner. The app is not offered to the public, and other people cannot sign in to it or connect their accounts.

What GTA6 Clipper does not do

  • It does not generate, edit or alter images or videos of people with AI. All video comes unchanged from real, permission-cleared gameplay streams; the only edits are trimming, cropping, captions and a credit line.
  • It does not create or upload sexual, intimate or nude content of any kind, including AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII).
  • It does not upload content from creators who have not given permission. Any creator can ask for their clips to be removed, and they will be taken down.
  • It does not access other people's Google or YouTube accounts, and it does not sell, share or use Google user data for advertising or AI model training.
